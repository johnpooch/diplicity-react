# Diplicity Code of Conduct

## Code of Conduct

Diplomacy is a game of negotiation, trust and betrayal. On Diplicity you can lie, scheme and backstab as ruthlessly as you like, but you can't mistreat fellow players beyond this or cheat.

This code applies everywhere on Diplicity: game chat, press, player profiles, usernames and game names.

## 1. Part of the game: Acceptable

These are how Diplomacy is played. Reports for them will be closed without action.

- **Lying and deception.** Promising support you never give, feeding false information, bluffing about your orders.
- **Breaking alliances.** Stabbing an ally, however long you've worked together and however late in the game.
- **Playing to win.** Ganging up on the leader, eliminating a weak player, refusing a draw, or holding out for a solo.
- **Choosing who to talk to.** Ignoring someone's messages, refusing to negotiate, or negotiating only with some players.
- **Hard bargaining.** Ultimatums and in-game threats, like "support me into Munich or I take Belgium".
- **Strategic choices you disagree with.** Throwing your weight behind another player, kingmaking, or playing badly.

## 2. Bad conduct and cheating: Reportable

### Abuse and harassment

Don't insult or demean other players. Never use slurs or attack anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or age. Historical or role-play framing is not an excuse. Be civil.

### Multiple accounts

Don't use a second account to play more than one power in the same game.

### Secret teaming

Don't enter a game with a friend or anyone who you have a connection with outside of the game and secretly coordinate as a team.

**Note:** Missing orders or abandoning a game is tracked automatically by the reliability system, so there is no need to report it.

## Reporting

If someone violates the code of conduct, report them and pick the reason that fits. Add a short note on what happened.

Reports are reviewed by the moderation team. Depending on what happened, a player may get a warning or be banned. Reports are confidential, and the reported player isn't told who made them.

Please don't report things in Part 1. False or retaliatory reports are themselves a breach of this code.
